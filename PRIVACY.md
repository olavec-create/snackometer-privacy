# Snackometer Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Snackometer is a novelty iPhone app that turns workout calories from Apple Health into snack equivalents and a shareable Snackostats image.

## Information Snackometer Reads

With your permission, Snackometer reads recent workout information from Apple Health, including workout type, workout date, workout duration, and active calories burned. Snackometer uses this information only to calculate and display snack equivalents inside the app.

## Information Snackometer Stores

Snackometer stores your selected snack preference and recent app state locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage. Snackometer does not create an account for you and does not operate a server-side user database.

## Sharing

When you choose to share Snackostats, Snackometer creates an image on your device and opens Apple's share sheet. The image is shared only with the apps or recipients you choose. Those destination apps and services are governed by their own privacy practices.

## What Snackometer Does Not Collect

Snackometer does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with the developer. Snackometer does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or remote logging.

## Apple Health Data

Apple Health data remains protected by Apple's Health permissions. You can grant, deny, or revoke Snackometer's access to Health data at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Children

Snackometer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if Snackometer's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted at this same URL.
